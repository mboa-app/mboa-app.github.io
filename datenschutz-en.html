Privacy policy

1. Controller

Jörg Neumann c/o Impressumservice Dein-Impressum Stettiner Str. 41 35410 Hungen

Email: meetusther26@gmail.com

2. Principle

We only store what Mboa needs. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Mboa does not handle payments.

3. Using the app without an account

You can use the map and list without an account. Your location is only used on your device to show listings near you and is not sent to us. Settings stay on your device.

4. Account (only for adding and reporting)

To add or report listings you sign in with your email address and a one-time code. We store your email address and sign-in time. Your email is never shown. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. Your listings (public)

What you add is visible to all app users: title, description, category, prices, languages, photos, Instagram/TikTok profile, your WhatsApp number and the location. Individuals can show their location only approximately (rounded to about 1 km). Do not upload photos of other people without their consent. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for optional details your consent (lit. a), which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the listing.

6. Reports

When you report a listing, we store which account reported which listing and when. After three reports a listing is hidden and reviewed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – preventing abuse).

7. Service providers

Database, sign-in and photo storage: Supabase Inc., servers in Frankfurt (EU), under a data processing agreement.

Maps: Google Maps on Android (Google Ireland Ltd.), Apple Maps on iPhone (Apple Inc.); in some cases OpenStreetMap maps via OpenFreeMap. Loading a map transmits technically necessary data such as your IP address to the map provider.

Contact via WhatsApp: tapping “Ask via WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.), whose privacy policy applies.

App delivery: Expo (650 Industries, Inc.); website and explainer video: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

8. Retention

Listings and your account are stored until you delete them. Deleted data is removed immediately; provider backups are overwritten shortly after.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account and listings yourself in the app (gear icon → Delete account). You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

10. Age

Mboa is intended for people aged 16 and over.
