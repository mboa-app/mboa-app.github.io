Privacy policy

Jörg Neumann c/o Impressumservice Dein-Impressum Stettiner Str. 41 35410 Hungen

Email: kontakt@wemanage.de

2. Principle

We only store what Mboa needs. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Mboa does not handle payments.

3. Using the app without an account

You can use the map and list without an account. Your location is only used on your device to show listings near you and is not sent to us. Settings (language, country, radius) stay on your device.

Detecting your country: so the map shows offers in your country, the app asks your phone’s location service (Apple or Google) once per start for the country of your location – rounded to about 10 km. You can also choose the country yourself in the settings.

4. Account (only for adding and reporting)

To add or report listings you sign in with your email address and a one-time code. We store your email address and sign-in time. Your email is never shown. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. Your listings (public)

What you add is visible to all app users: title, description, category, prices, languages, photos, Instagram/TikTok profile, your WhatsApp number, the location and the date of the listing (for the “New on Mboa” label). Individuals can show their location only approximately (rounded to about 1 km). For shops, churches and associations with an exact location, the street and number are shown too – never for individuals. Do not upload photos of other people without their consent. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for optional details your consent (lit. a), which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the listing.

6. Reports

When you report a listing, we store which account reported which listing and when. After three reports a listing is hidden and reviewed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – preventing abuse).

7. Service providers

Database, sign-in and photo storage: Supabase Inc., servers in Frankfurt (EU), under a data processing agreement.

Automatic check of new listings: to keep spam and fraud off the map, the title, description, category, location, street (businesses only), services, details, website and linked profile of new or changed listings are checked automatically by Mistral AI SAS (Paris, France; processing in the EU) under a data processing agreement. Your WhatsApp number, email address and photos are not transmitted (numbers and email addresses in the text are removed first), and the data is not used for training. Mistral keeps requests only briefly for abuse monitoring (at most 30 days). The result is only a hint for our review; whether a listing is hidden is always decided by a person. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (preventing abuse).

Place search when adding a listing: when you search for a place, set the pin or the app uses your location (if you allowed it), the app looks up the town and, for businesses, the street via your phone’s location service (Apple or Google; in the web preview OpenStreetMap/Nominatim). The search term or the map point is transmitted – rounded if you choose “approximate” and when your location is used automatically. Only what is in the listing is stored.

Location service for country and place: your phone’s location service (Apple Inc. or Google), see section 3.

Maps: Google Maps on Android (Google Ireland Ltd.), Apple Maps on iPhone (Apple Inc.); in some cases OpenStreetMap maps via OpenFreeMap. Loading a map transmits technically necessary data such as your IP address to the map provider.

Contact via WhatsApp: tapping “Ask via WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.), whose privacy policy applies.

App delivery: Expo (650 Industries, Inc.); website and explainer video: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

8. Retention

Listings and your account are stored until you delete them. Deleted data is removed immediately; provider backups are overwritten shortly after.

9. Your rights

If you use Mboa outside the EU, for example in the United Kingdom, the local data protection laws (e.g. UK GDPR) also apply; your rights are the same, and in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent. You can delete your account and listings yourself in the app (gear icon → Delete account). You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

10. Age

Mboa is intended for people aged 16 and over.
