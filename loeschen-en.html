Delete account and data

In the app

Open Mboa, tap the gear icon at the top right, then “Delete account”. Your account, all your listings, photos and reports are deleted immediately.

By email

Write to meetusther26@gmail.com from or mentioning your account email. We delete everything within 30 days and confirm it.

What is deleted

Email address and sign-in data, all listings with photos, WhatsApp number and location, and your reports. Settings on your device are removed by uninstalling the app.
