Terms of use

1. What this is

Mboa is a free map where the African and Caribbean community shows its offers – in Germany and the other countries where Mboa is available. Mboa only puts people in touch. Providers and customers agree on prices and payment directly; Mboa is not involved and accepts no liability for it.

Order in the list: community offers within 30 km first, then all listings by distance. Listings added by users in the last 30 days are marked “New on Mboa”. Nobody can buy a better position.

2. Allowed listings

Real offers only: hairstyles, braids and beauty, food and catering, events and celebrations (decoration, photos, DJ, rentals), products, churches, shops, associations and meeting places. Information must be true. Anyone offering services regularly for money is responsible for business registration, taxes and hygiene rules.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Not allowed: fraud and fake offers, hate, discrimination, harassment, violence, sexual or offensive content, illegal goods and services, gambling, investment or loan offers, spam, and photos of other people without their consent. Such listings are removed and the account is blocked.

4. Reporting and hiding

Anyone can report a listing via “Report wrong listing”; it is hidden for that person immediately. After three reports it is hidden for everyone and reviewed. We usually act on reports within 24 hours. You can also email kontakt@wemanage.de.

Illegal content (e.g. fraud, sexual services, illegal goods): we review such notices without delay, remove the listings and block the account. Point of contact for users and authorities (also under the EU Digital Services Act and the UK Online Safety Act): kontakt@wemanage.de, in English, French or German.

5. Limits

Up to 3 listings per account. We may delete listings that break these rules without notice.
