Terms of use

1. What this is

Mboa is a free map where the West African community in Germany shows its offers. Mboa only puts people in touch. Providers and customers agree on prices and payment directly; Mboa is not involved and accepts no liability for it.

2. Allowed listings

Real offers only: hairstyles and braids, food and catering, products, churches, shops, associations and meeting places. Information must be true. Anyone offering services regularly for money is responsible for business registration, taxes and hygiene rules.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Not allowed: fraud and fake offers, hate, discrimination, harassment, violence, sexual or offensive content, illegal goods and services, gambling, investment or loan offers, spam, and photos of other people without their consent. Such listings are removed and the account is blocked.

4. Reporting and hiding

Anyone can report a listing via “Report wrong listing”; it is hidden for that person immediately. After three reports it is hidden for everyone and reviewed. We usually act on reports within 24 hours. You can also email meetusther26@gmail.com.

5. Limits

Up to 3 listings per account. We may delete listings that break these rules without notice.
